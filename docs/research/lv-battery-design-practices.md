The design inputs come from Formula Student Oulu's M06H Aurora brief. ## 4.1 Cell compression for pouch cells

**Published evidence.** Aufschläger et al. cycled 5 Ah NMC622/graphite pouches under flexible and rigid compression at 75, 200, 500, 1,000 and 1,750 kPa using cell-specific multi-step fast-charge profiles. They recommended 200–500 kPa for their two cell types as a life/DCIR/swelling trade-off. Flexible loading averaged 19% longer life to 80% capacity retention than fixed thickness, and irreversible swelling fell from about 3.2% to 2.1% of initial uncompressed thickness in the moderate-load comparison. Excess pressure also increased charging overpotential and plating risk. A separate study measured reversible swelling during a 0.05C charge from 3.0 to 4.2 V at 20 °C. No good source found for the EP pouch's approved pressure or reversible and end-of-life swelling. [Aufschläger et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.est.2023.109883) [Aufschläger et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352152X22024720) [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md)

**HSC v3.** Keep the two plate-and-tie-rod stacks if the plates stay flat and the cell load bypasses the tabs. Tie rods allow independent preload measurement and stack removal. A strap saves hardware but needs proof against edge loading and tension loss. An enclosure that doubles as a fixed stop saves separate plates but couples crash/assembly tolerances to pouch compression. Use a compliant layer with a measured force window for either alternative. EV5.5.12–.15 supply useful benchmarks for the HSC: at least 80% of each used large face, compliant compensation, high-current path mechanically unloaded, and limits of 1.0 MPa compression and 0.05 MPa shear or lower datasheet values, using 80% of nominal face area for the EV calculation. These are EV design benchmarks here. [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md) [FSG, 2027 v1.0](https://www.formulastudent.de/fileadmin/user_upload/all/2027/rules/FS_Rules_2027_v1.0.pdf) [Aufschläger et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.est.2023.109883) [Technical University of Liberec, 2026](https://theses.cz/id/rl9ziz/)

- Obtain stress versus strain, temperature and aged compression set data for the exact PORON polyurethane or BISCO silicone grade. Check the actual stack-up including fins, insulating film and production tolerances. Select a pad working range that holds the supplier's minimum and maximum pressure across assembly tolerance, reversible swelling and life growth. Apply cell thickness changes across all 14 or 12 physical cells in a loaded stack, as applicable to the final geometry. Keep NMC swelling data as a sensitivity case only. [Rogers, PORON battery-pad application note](https://rogerscorp.com/-/media/project/rogerscorp/documents/elastomeric-material-solutions/poron/english/application-notes/battery-pad-application-note.pdf) [Rogers, BISCO sealing technical bulletin](https://www.rogerscorp.com/-/media/project/rogerscorp/documents/elastomeric-material-solutions/general/english/technical-information/180-454-battery-pack-sealing-solutions-technical-bulletin.pdf) [Aufschläger et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.est.2023.109883) [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md)
- Measure plate load using a calibrated compression load cell and divide the measured force by loaded area. Use appropriately ranged film for pressure distribution and edge peaks on a representative stack. Log stack height and force at assembly, at temperature/SOC extremes and after dwell or cycling. Set production acceptance from those measurements and the supplier limits. [Aufschläger et al., 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352152X22024720) [Tekscan, Prescale pressure film](https://www.tekscan.com/products-solutions/pressure-sensing-film/fujifilm-prescale-film) [Rogers, PORON battery-pad application note](https://rogerscorp.com/-/media/project/rogerscorp/documents/elastomeric-material-solutions/poron/english/application-notes/battery-pad-application-note.pdf)

## 4.2 Thermal design for air-cooled packs

**Published evidence.** NREL describes the packaging and thermal-uniformity trade-offs of air cooling. Parallel-flow pack research optimizes manifold geometry to balance passages, while Dynamis PRC used a cell thermal model tied to endurance data. FST Lisboa used airflow over tabs and one pouch face in its EV design. [NREL, 1999](https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy99osti/25992.pdf) [Applied Thermal Engineering, parallel-airflow study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1359431117324845) [Dynamis PRC / Politecnico di Milano, 2022](https://www.politesi.polimi.it/handle/10589/186378) [Sánchez / FST Lisboa, 2024 extended abstract](https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/downloadFile/1126295043841613/DianaSanchez_IST193234_Extended_Abstract.pdf)

**HSC v3.** Compare the existing aluminium spreader fins with direct gap flow on one representative stack. Fins preserve a separate conduction path but add interface resistance, mass and a conductive surface to isolate. Direct gap flow puts air against more cell area but needs passages that remain open at full swell and preserve retention. Feed the 7- and 6-pair stacks from a shared plenum with parallel paths. Seal bypass gaps and map flow near the first, middle and last branches of each stack. In serial flow, downstream air starts warmer. In parallel flow, the limiting branch may still be the low-flow branch. [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md) [NREL, 1999](https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy99osti/25992.pdf) [Applied Thermal Engineering, parallel-airflow study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1359431117324845) [Sánchez / FST Lisboa, 2024 extended abstract](https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/downloadFile/1126295043841613/DianaSanchez_IST193234_Extended_Abstract.pdf) [Henkel, SIL PAD TSP K900](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/us/en/products/thermal-management-materials/central-pdp.html/bergquist-sil-pad-tsp-k900/112196IB.html)

- Use shrouded fins and a documented thin dielectric thermal interface. Compare thermal impedance at representative pressure, isolation and abrasion after cycling. Use an adhesive as a thermal interface only if its cured bond-line impedance, electrical properties and durability are documented. Keep adhesive thickness for sensing separate from the fin interface. [Henkel, SIL PAD TSP K900](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/us/en/products/thermal-management-materials/central-pdp.html/bergquist-sil-pad-tsp-k900/112196IB.html) [FSG, 2027 v1.0](https://www.formulastudent.de/fileadmin/user_upload/all/2027/rules/FS_Rules_2027_v1.0.pdf)
- Size the radial blower at the intersection of its pressure–flow curve with the installed duct system resistance. Measure flow or pressure at low 12 V supply, at standstill and with guards installed. Air-side first pass: mass flow ≈ pack heat ÷ (air specific heat × allowed air rise). For example, 200 W and 10 K with air at approximately 1 kJ/(kg·K) gives 0.020 kg/s. This is a sizing example, with actual heat and flow to be measured. [ebm-papst, fan FAQ](https://www.ebmpapst.com/us/en/support/faq.html) [NREL, 1999](https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy99osti/25992.pdf) [Bernardi et al., 1985](https://scixplorer.org/abs/1985JElS..132....5B/abstract)
- Model one thermal capacitance per representative cell or pair and one heat-to-air resistance per path. Start with irreversible I²R using pulse DCIR at relevant SOC and temperature, add joint losses and calibrate against load data. Include reversible heating or model residuals where material. Log inlet, outlet, pouch face and negative tab temperatures. Use ≤5 °C hottest-to-coldest as a provisional development target and set the hottest-cell operating target below the lower of the supplier bound and the rule's 60 °C ceiling, with measured lag and uncertainty margin. No good source found for a chemistry-specific ΔT acceptance limit for EP9343126VVP. [Bernardi et al., 1985](https://scixplorer.org/abs/1985JElS..132....5B/abstract) [USABC / Idaho National Laboratory, 2025](https://uscar.org/download/247/usabc-manuals/16391/electric-vehicle-test-manual.pdf) [Dynamis PRC / Politecnico di Milano, 2022](https://www.politesi.polimi.it/handle/10589/186378) [FSG, 2027 v1.0](https://www.formulastudent.de/fileadmin/user_upload/all/2027/rules/FS_Rules_2027_v1.0.pdf) [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md)

## 4.3 Busbars and tab joints

**Published evidence.** The Copper Development Association guide covers contact resistance, pressure distribution, surface treatment and bolted-joint behavior under thermal cycling. Ultrasonic weld experiments measured resistance and temperature rise on tab/busbar coupons. Laser weld experiments characterized mechanical, metallurgical, electrical and thermal behavior across Al and Cu combinations, showing why the process must be qualified for the actual stack. FST Lisboa provides a Formula Student pouch packaging reference. No good source found for a validated clamp pressure or allowable joint resistance for the EP tabs, or for a public FSG HY clamped-tab life test. [Copper Development Association, Copper for Busbars](https://kupfer.de/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Copper-for-Busbars.pdf) [Das et al., ultrasonic tab-joint characterization](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352152X18307047) [Journal of Energy Storage, welded Al/Cu joints](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352152X24033474) [Dual-mode laser tab-to-busbar welding study, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666330924000669) [Sánchez / FST Lisboa, 2024 extended abstract](https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/downloadFile/1126295043841613/DianaSanchez_IST193234_Extended_Abstract.pdf) [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md)

**HSC v3.** Keep the removable aluminium push-bar concept if samples pass. Use a broad, flat clamp and a separate support for the busbar. Drill the hardware rather than the live tabs unless the cell manufacturer approves a tab-hole drawing. A bolted clamp is serviceable and can be checked with a torque record. Ultrasonic or laser welding trades disassembly for a permanent joint and requires supplier approval, tooling and coupon qualification. Compare resistance, pull strength and heat damage on representative Al and Cu tab stacks before choosing a weld. CV5.1.8 imports EV4.5.15, which excludes soldering cells or wires in the high-current path. Adopt high-temperature positive locking and photograph inaccessible joints, drawing on EV4.5.13–.14 and checking T10 for critical hardware. [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md) [FSG, 2027 v1.0](https://www.formulastudent.de/fileadmin/user_upload/all/2027/rules/FS_Rules_2027_v1.0.pdf) [Copper Development Association, Copper for Busbars](https://kupfer.de/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Copper-for-Busbars.pdf) [Das et al., ultrasonic tab-joint characterization](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352152X18307047) [Journal of Energy Storage, welded Al/Cu joints](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352152X24033474) [Dual-mode laser tab-to-busbar welding study, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666330924000669)

- Compare Al for lower mass, Cu for lower bulk resistance at equal geometry, and plated Cu where a qualified plating system addresses the mating surface. Confirm tab metallurgy and coating first. For dissimilar metals, qualify the exact interface for moisture/corrosion and electrical stability. Follow the supplier's cleaning and oxide-control procedure and avoid residues. Record surface prep, flatness and assembly torque. Keep pressure with stack-up design and approved hardware, and check creep and preload loss after soak and vibration. [Copper Development Association, Copper for Busbars](https://kupfer.de/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Copper-for-Busbars.pdf) [Journal of Energy Storage, welded Al/Cu joints](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352152X24033474) [FSO task brief, 2026](https://github.com/mestariwokki/FSS-HS-SIMULATOR/blob/main/docs/codex-tasks/lv-battery-design-research.md)
- Define a heat budget and work back using R ≤ P/I². For a full-current joint with an illustrative 1 W local budget at 200 A, the target is ≤25 µΩ, which dissipates about 2.7 W during a 330 A pulse. Confirm those assumptions with temperature rise and branch-current data. Kelvin-measure defined points across each joint, using the same probe positions and temperature before and after cycling. Check each tab branch as well as the common path. ## 4.4 Fusing and protection

**Rule and evidence.** T11.7.6 places short-circuit protection within 100 mm of ungrounded terminals. T11.7.7 requires overcurrent protection that trips at or below the cells' specified maximum discharge current. Eaton distinguishes continuous loading, time-current behavior, DC interrupt rating, and clearing I²t. CV5.2.2 requires the normally open mechanical AIR on positive inside the HSC, and CV4.1.4 specifies its coil interface to the SDC and hybrid controller. [FSG, 2027 v1.0](https://www.formulastudent.de/fileadmin/user_upload/all/2027/rules/FS_Rules_2027_v1.0.pdf) [Eaton, EV fuse selection guide](https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/products/electronic-components/resources/technical/eaton-ev-fuse-selection-guide-elx1527-en.pdf)

**HSC v3.**

1. Start from verified per-cell current versus time, temperature and SOC. Use the matching test and branch measurements to account for unequal sharing in 2P. Overlay logged discharge and regen, manufacturer fuse min-melt and total-clear curves, and cell/wire/contact thermal limits. Derate for enclosure temperature and pulse repetition. Select DC voltage rating for the maximum application voltage and DC interrupt rating above prospective short current, with the manufacturer's applicable circuit conditions. Check clearing I²t against downstream withstand and heating. Resolve the rule's allowable continuous/pulse interpretation in writing. A nominal 200 A fuse can carry above its rating for a time. If the necessary protection and endurance duty overlap, reduce operational current. Keep a short protected routing drawing with dimension and terminal references for inspection. 2. The main fuse handles pack-path overcurrent. One branch can feed its parallel neighbour during a local fault, even if the main circuit is open. Evaluate an engineered cell-branch fusible link only if discharge, regen, minimum fault current and aged pulse withstand provide a demonstrated selective window. A series group fuse cannot isolate circulating current within its own 2P group. For the specific AEV250 suffix, check coil voltage, economizer and dropout, mounting, thermal rise inside the case, rated DC make/break at the measured voltage and current in both directions, and fuse coordination. 4. Measure both ESCs' combined capacitance and standby load. For a switched resistor and an initially discharged ideal link, initial current is V/R, resistor pulse energy is ½CV² and 95% voltage is reached in about 3RC. Treat EV5.7's 95% as a useful benchmark for this hybrid.
